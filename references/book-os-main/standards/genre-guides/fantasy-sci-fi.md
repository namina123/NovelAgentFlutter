# Fantasy/Science Fiction Style Guide

## Genre Characteristics

### World Building
- Establish clear rules for magic/technology systems
- Create consistent internal logic for fantastical elements
- Integrate world-building naturally into narrative flow
- Avoid info-dumping; reveal world details through story action

### Speculative Elements
- Ground fantastical elements in relatable human experiences
- Use speculation to explore themes and human nature
- Maintain consistency in speculative rules throughout
- Balance wonder with believability

### Pacing and Exposition
- Introduce world-building elements gradually
- Use character discovery to reveal world details
- Balance action with necessary exposition
- Keep focus on character goals and conflicts

## Technical World Building

### Magic/Technology Systems
- Define clear rules and limitations
- Show consequences of using fantastical elements
- Create costs or prices for magical/technological power
- Maintain consistency in how systems work

### Setting Integration
- Make setting an active part of the story
- Use environment to create obstacles and opportunities
- Show how characters adapt to or struggle with their world
- Integrate cultural and social elements naturally

## Character Development in Speculative Fiction

### Character Grounding
- Root characters in recognizable human emotions and motivations
- Show how fantastical circumstances affect normal people
- Use character reactions to establish world strangeness or normalcy
- Balance character agency with world constraints

### Cultural Integration
- Show how characters fit within their world's culture
- Use dialogue and behavior to reveal cultural norms
- Create believable social structures and hierarchies
- Address how speculative elements affect daily life

## Narrative Techniques

### Balancing Wonder and Story
- Use fantastical elements to serve character and plot
- Create sense of wonder without overwhelming narrative
- Ground speculation in emotional truth
- Use familiar story structures with speculative twists

### Exposition Strategies
- Reveal world information through character experience
- Use dialogue naturally to convey necessary information
- Show world rules through character actions and consequences
- Avoid lengthy explanatory passages; prefer demonstration
