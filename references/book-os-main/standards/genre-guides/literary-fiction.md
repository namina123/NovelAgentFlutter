# Literary Fiction Style Guide

## Genre Characteristics

### Thematic Focus
- Explore universal human experiences and emotions
- Prioritize character development over plot complexity
- Address social, psychological, or philosophical themes
- Emphasize internal conflict and character psychology

### Prose Style
- Emphasize beautiful, crafted language
- Use metaphor and symbolism meaningfully
- Vary sentence structure for rhythm and flow
- Focus on precise word choice and imagery

### Pacing and Structure
- Allow for slower, more contemplative pacing
- Use introspection and reflection appropriately
- Build tension through character relationships and internal conflict
- Structure around character revelations and emotional beats

## Character Development

### Psychological Depth
- Develop complex, flawed, relatable characters
- Explore character motivations and internal contradictions
- Show character growth through subtle changes
- Use backstory to inform present character actions

### Relationships
- Focus on relationship dynamics and emotional complexity
- Explore how characters affect and change each other
- Use dialogue to reveal character depth and relationship tension
- Show rather than tell relationship evolution

## Literary Techniques

### Symbolism and Metaphor
- Use symbols that emerge naturally from the story
- Employ metaphors that enhance rather than distract
- Layer meaning without being heavy-handed
- Connect symbols to character arcs and themes

### Language and Voice
- Develop a distinctive narrative voice
- Use language appropriate to character and setting
- Employ literary devices purposefully
- Balance beautiful prose with clear storytelling
